CROSS-ASSET CORRELATION // SCENARIO MODEL

Matrix values & method

Normalized factor covariance: liquidity, fees, derivatives, dilution plus independent residual risk. Slider magnitude increases the variance of the corresponding factor. Exposures are illustrative assumptions, not historical return estimates. Both shock directions increase factor stress. SOL exposures: liquidity 1.25, fees 0.80, derivatives 1.35, dilution -0.55; independent residual variance 0.60.

Scenario formulas & limitations

TVL′ = TVL × (1 + TVL shock). MCAP′ = MCAP × (1 + unlock pressure), assuming constant token price. Fees′ = observed daily fees × (1 + fee shock). OI′ = OI × (1 + derivatives shock). Funding′ = funding + shock × 0.0001, with shock expressed as a fraction. Fees are daily observations, not fabricated 30-day totals.

Score = weighted mean of available components: TVL/MCAP (35%), daily fees/P25 (25%), 24h volume/$1M (20%), funding proximity to 0.0001 (20%), or annualized measured daily fees/MCAP for spot-only assets without funding observations. Each component is capped to [0,1]; subtract unlock pressure × 25 points. Missing components are omitted and force MONITOR unless a known invalidation already occurs. Buffer = score − (50 − margin) points. ACTIVE requires buffer ≥ 10 and complete metrics; buffer < 0 is INVALIDATED. Derivatives also invalidate above funding 0.0001 or a simulated OI drop of 20% (explicit scenario assumption). Trap strategies monitor the affected protocol's resilience, not the profitability of a short position.

This transparent sensitivity model does not rerun the source SQLite thesis expressions, predict returns, or establish historical performance. Frame updates use requestAnimationFrame; actual frame rate depends on the device.
