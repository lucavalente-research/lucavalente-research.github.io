How I reason

What the data shows, and what I’m watching.

What I observe

  • Real usage: what people actually do on a protocol.
  • Fees: what they pay to use it.
  • Liquidity: the money that sits in it.
  • Derivatives: open interest and funding on futures.
  • Flows: where money leaves and where it arrives.
  • Each protocol next to similar ones: chains with chains, exchanges with exchanges.

The measures, one page each

  • Data check: whether a number rests on a single odd day.
  • Fee quality: whether fees are earned every day or on a few one-off days.
  • Who’s winning: who gained and who lost share among sector peers in a month.
  • Code & data: the open-source methods and the daily numbers.

How I explain it

  • What the data shows, with its date and its public source.
  • What is worth watching next.
