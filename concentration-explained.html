Concentration: one chain, a few days

What it measures

  • How much a protocol depends on one chain, or on a few days.
  • The concentration index is the sum of the squared shares: near 0 when the total is spread evenly, 1 when it all sits in one place.
  • It is applied to value locked by chain and to fees by day.

How to read it

  • One divided by the index reads as the number of equal parts that would look the same: an index of 0.38 is like 2.7 equal chains.
  • For fees by day the index is rescaled, so that 0 means the same amount every day.
  • Chain figures are left empty when the chain breakdown is missing or does not add up to the protocol’s value locked within 5%.

What it does not say

  • A high index is a description, not a verdict: a protocol built for one chain is concentrated by design.
  • Volume by trading pair is not covered: the sources used do not publish it as a daily series.
