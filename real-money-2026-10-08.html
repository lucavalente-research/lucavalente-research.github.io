Reading

In the 30 days to 8 Oct 2026 Marinade Liquid Staking's value locked changed by +9.6%: +12% from token prices and −2.7% from net deposits. The price effect is the quantities held 30 days earlier times the change in each token's price; net deposits are the change in quantities at today's prices. The two parts add up to the change in value locked.
