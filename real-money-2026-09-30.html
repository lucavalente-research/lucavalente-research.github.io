Meta Pool Near: value locked up 160% in 30 days, deposits down 9.5%

RocketSwap Anubis took in the most new deposits in 30 days: +126% of its value locked

Reading

In the 30 days to 30 Sep 2026 Meta Pool Near's value locked changed by +160%: +169% from token prices and −9.5% from net deposits. The price effect is the quantities held 30 days earlier times the change in each token's price; net deposits are the change in quantities at today's prices. The two parts add up to the change in value locked. Across protocols above $10M, RocketSwap Anubis had the largest net deposits relative to its size: +126%.
