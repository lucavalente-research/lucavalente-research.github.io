Who’s winning: shares among peers

What it measures

  • Each protocol’s share of a 30-day total, such as trading volume or fees, among its sector peers.
  • The chart shows how that share moved in a month: who gained and who lost.
  • Shares are computed on 30-day totals, with one protocol per family.

How to read it

  • A share is the protocol’s part of the total across the peers compared, so the shares add up to 100% inside the group.
  • A change is in percentage points over a month: from 26% to 36% is 10 points.
  • A protocol can grow and still lose share when its peers grow faster.
  • No comparison is made in groups of fewer than five members.

What it does not say

  • A larger share is not a verdict on quality.
  • It says nothing about token prices.
  • It describes the last 30 days against the 30 days before.
