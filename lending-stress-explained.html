Lending stress: how much of the deposits is lent out

What it measures

  • For each market of Aave v3 and Morpho: deposits, borrowed amount, available liquidity and the two interest rates, as the protocol publishes them.
  • Utilization is the borrowed amount divided by deposits.
  • A market is highlighted when it holds at least $10M of deposits, is not frozen, and utilization is above 90% (above 99% for Morpho, whose markets are built to sit at 90%).

How to read it

  • It is a picture of one moment, with its time stated on the chart. Utilization moves minute by minute.
  • Lending markets are designed to keep utilization near a set level, so a market near that level is where its design puts it.
  • Charts dated before 2 October 2026 highlight Morpho markets above 90% as well: the 99% line for Morpho applies from that date.

What it does not say

  • It does not say what happens next.
  • Highlighted means that most deposits were lent out at that moment, nothing more.
