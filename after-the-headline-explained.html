After the headline: what followed an event

What it measures

  • What followed an event, such as a governance vote or a security incident, compared with the rest of the sector.
  • The change after the event is set against the median change of sector peers over the same days, so a move shared by the whole sector does not count.
  • Value locked compares the level some days later with the median of the 5 days before; fees and transactions compare the daily average after with the average of the 7 days before.

How to read it

  • The gap is in percentage points: the protocol’s change minus the median change of its peers.
  • For a type of event the gaps of many events are averaged, with a 90% interval. Below 15 events no average is given.

What it does not say

  • Same period is not proven cause: other things happen on the same days.
  • Survivors only: protocols that disappeared after an incident are not in today’s list, so the averages understate what incidents can do.
  • Peers are a blunt control: the sector median removes market-wide moves, not everything else.
